# What the real complaints look like

Source: CFPB Consumer Complaint Database (public). Complaints with a consumer narrative and a closed outcome.

- **Complaints:** 24,000 from 2024-01-01 to 2026-05-31, 1,215 companies, 8 products, 79 issues
- **Outcome mix:** explanation 86.3%, non_monetary_relief 7.8%, monetary_relief 5.9%
- **Narrative length:** median 165 words, 90th percentile 445 words
- **Reading time alone:** 376 analyst-hours for this sample at 238 words/min (0.9 min per complaint before any research)
- **Priority floor from risk signals:** low 52.6%, medium 34.8%, high 12.5%
- **Risk signals (share of complaints):** fraud_or_identity_theft 25.6%, legal_threat 8.4%, financial_hardship 7.8%, older_american 7.5%, servicemember 12.1%
